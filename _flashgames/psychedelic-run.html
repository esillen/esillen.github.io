Psychedelic Run

Fly through gates that match your color. Click the game to focus it before using the keyboard.

Controls for two players

Player 1: arrow keys to move, Shift to shoot, Ctrl for a grenade.

Player 2: I, J, K, L to move, A to shoot, D for a grenade.

If the player does not load, download the SWF and open it with Ruffle Desktop.
